## Implementation interpretation

The open protocol preserves claims and their authors without an admission gate. Opportunity matching helps complementary roles find each other. Contract policies determine which proofs or explicit judgments authorize token issuance. Source infrastructure and specialized projects have separate ownership records, with a disclosed revenue route between them. Contribution credit survives ownership transfers.

One percent is an implemented scenario with explicit dilution math and bounds. It is not a permanent one-percent entitlement for every account or an assertion that every contribution has equal value. Model verdicts must identify the model, criteria, evidence and authority they have been given. Dataset use requires separate permission; joining a project does not imply consent to train a model.
