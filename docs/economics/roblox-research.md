# What Koinos can learn from Roblox

Research checked on 29 October 2026. These observations inform design hypotheses; they do not establish that copying a payout percentage will reproduce Roblox's outcomes.

Roblox combines creation tools with hosting, distribution, discovery and monetization. Its 2025 annual filing reports $1.503 billion earned by creators, with more than 23,500 creators receiving fiat payouts that year. The same filing reports an operating loss, so creator payout growth is not proof that the whole economic system is profitable. [2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm).

DevEx converts eligible earned Robux into local currency subject to program requirements. It distinguishes value earned through creation from several other ways an account can receive Robux. This is a useful distinction between contributions that qualify for a reward and merely holding or transferring units. It is an administered program, not permissionless settlement. [Developer Exchange](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange).

Creator Rewards separately rewards daily engagement and audience expansion. Its documented audience-expansion terms use eligibility conditions, measurement periods and anti-abuse rules. The design lesson is to define the measured outcome and abuse model before promising a payout. Koinos should not treat self-reported activity as sales or useful work. [Creator Rewards](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-rewards).

| Observed mechanism | Koinos design hypothesis | What must be measured |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tools and distribution in one ecosystem | Exo helps people build; an open hub helps them find collaborators and demand. | Time to first accepted contribution and first real customer. |
| Rewards for creation and audience growth | Credit product work, maintenance and market access under separate criteria. | Incremental value, repeat business, fraud and concentration. |
| Shared services support many creators | A disclosed branch fee funds source infrastructure and its contributors. | Service cost, reliability, fee burden and creator retention. |
| Explicit payout eligibility | Fixed project policies make acceptance and appeals inspectable. | False acceptance, false rejection, appeal outcomes and payout delay. |

Koinos differs in ownership and portability: contribution records can move between tools, reward rules can be committed in contracts, source and branch ownership can be distinct, and a participant can leave with their evidence. Those features are engineering choices to test, not proof that a marketplace will attract buyers.

Subsidizing agent inference could lower the cost of starting projects. It also creates a budget and abuse problem. Any pilot should have explicit per-project limits, approved expenditure, measured outcomes and a path to operating without subsidies. The reference does not spend funds on inference.
