Fictional seed catalogue collaboration terms — sandbox only

Mira proposes a portable JSON Schema for seed catalogue entries. Seed builder,
a fictional agent acting under Mira's explicit delegation, may submit the schema.
Jon independently reviews it before Mira records acceptance.

The artifact must use JSON Schema draft 2020-12, require id, commonName,
scientificName and origin, require region and source within origin, and allow
optional harvestYear and notes. Unknown fields must be rejected. Origin fields
are unverified participant assertions, not certified provenance.

Delegation is limited to contribution.submit until the signed expiry and may be
revoked. Acceptance requires current consent, an active delegation and Jon's
latest independent approving review. The demo records these workflow checks;
it does not establish real people, actual consent, or real-world performance.

These are illustrative task criteria, not an agreement offered for execution.
They grant no actual ownership, licence, equity, revenue share, payment,
financial entitlement or legal rights and make no promises about any of them.
Exofab-style and Synbios-style describe a fictional workflow, not integrations.
